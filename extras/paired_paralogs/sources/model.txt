********** MODEL NAME
Uridylylation of PII and GlnK with AmtB sequestration

********** MODEL NOTES
This model describes the dynamics of GlnB and GlnK uridylylation. It is based on the fact that UTase has two distinct active sites for which PII/GlnK and PII-U/GlnK-U compete respectively. Ternary complex between GlnB/GlnK, GlnB-U/GlnK-U and UTase are neglected. Both antagonistic directions of the UT/UR reaction follow Michaelean kinetics. Glutamine is taken as an allosteric activator of UR activity and inhibitor of UT activity. The total protein amount of GlnB, GlnK and GS vary dynamically, these are fed into the model at each time point. Furthermore the total GlnK available to the system is controlled by the level of aKG in order to model the effect of sequestration by GlnK.
